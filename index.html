Two Councils, One Campus

VIMS and VIDS are separate, independently-recognised colleges that share the Vydehi campus and hospital — not one merged institution.

This matters for admissions: VIMS (medical) answers to the National Medical Commission, VIDS (dental) to the Dental Council of India, and each runs its own NEET-based counselling track through KEA. The sections below cover each college on its own terms.

Programmes & Eligibility

MBBS, MD/MS, B.Sc., BDS and MDS — what each path actually requires

Every NEET-route programme below requires a valid NEET score at entry — UG, PG (MD/MS), super-speciality (DM/M.Ch) or MDS as applicable. There is no admission route at VIMS or VIDS that bypasses NEET, regardless of category or quota.

VIMS · MBBS

Bachelor of Medicine & Bachelor of Surgery

Duration: 4.5 years + 1-year internship Seats: 250 / year Entrance: NEET UG Eligibility: 10+2 PCB, 50% (40% SC/ST/OBC)
Counselling runs through the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) across government, private and NRI/management categories, following NMC and Karnataka fee-regulatory rules. Clinical training takes place inside the attached 1,600-bed multi-specialty hospital, which sees a high daily patient volume across general medicine, surgery, OBG, paediatrics and super-specialties like cardiology, neurology and oncology.
VIMS · MD / MS

Doctor of Medicine & Master of Surgery

Duration: 3 years Seats: 138 / year Entrance: NEET PG Eligibility: MBBS + completed internship
Postgraduate clinical and pre/para-clinical specialisations across departments including general medicine, general surgery, OBG, paediatrics, anaesthesiology, radiology, pathology, microbiology, pharmacology, community medicine, dermatology, psychiatry, orthopaedics, ophthalmology, ENT and hospital administration. Selection follows NEET PG rank through centralised PG counselling; seat distribution across specialisations varies year to year and should be confirmed against the current KEA/counselling-body seat matrix.
VIMS · DM / M.Ch

Super-Speciality Programmes

Duration: 3 years Seats: 25 / year Entrance: NEET SS Eligibility: MD/MS in the relevant parent specialty
Super-speciality training (DM for medical streams, M.Ch for surgical streams) in departments such as cardiology, cardio-thoracic & vascular surgery, neurology, neurosurgery, nephrology, gastroenterology, endocrinology, medical oncology, surgical oncology, plastic surgery and urology, drawing on VIMS's tertiary-care case volume.
VIMS · B.Sc. Allied Health Sciences

B.Sc. Nursing, OTT, Medical Imaging & Renal Dialysis Technology

Duration: 4 years (varies by stream) Entrance: NEET (from 2026 cycle) / merit, per course Eligibility: 10+2 PCB
VIMS offers Allied Health Science degrees in B.Sc. Nursing, B.Sc. Operation Theatre Technology, B.Sc. Medical Imaging Technology and B.Sc. Renal Dialysis Technology, with clinical postings inside the same 1,600-bed hospital used for MBBS and PG training. These run alongside the College of Nursing (VINS) within the Vydehi Group; confirm with the admissions office which programmes are administered under VIMS versus VINS for the current academic year, and whether NEET is required for your specific stream and intake year.
VIDS · BDS

Bachelor of Dental Surgery

Admission Process 2026-27

From NEET result to reporting at Vydehi

The process is identical in shape for MBBS, BDS and MDS — only the entrance exam and counselling body differ slightly. NEET UG feeds both MBBS and BDS counselling; NEET MDS feeds the dental postgraduate round.

STEP 1
Appear for NEET UG (MBBS/BDS) or NEET MDS
Conducted by the National Testing Agency / National Board of Examinations. No admission proceeds without a valid, current-year scorecard.
STEP 2
Register for KEA counselling
Karnataka Examination Authority handles centralised counselling for government, private and NRI/management seats at private colleges including Vydehi.
STEP 3
Document verification
Originals plus photocopies of marksheets, NEET scorecard, category and ID documents are verified at the designated KEA centre.
STEP 4
Choice filling — list VIMS or VIDS
Rank the specific college and course as a preference; seat allotment follows merit within category and quota.
STEP 5
Seat allotment (Round 1, Round 2, mop-up)
Allotment runs in rounds; unfilled seats move to a mop-up round, historically with the option to upgrade if eligible.
STEP 6
Report to the Vydehi campus, pay fees, enrol
Carry the allotment letter, original documents and the demand draft for first-year fees to the admissions office at 82, EPIP Area, Whitefield.
On management/NRI quota and fees: Both NMC (for MBBS) and the Karnataka fee regulatory framework still require a valid NEET score for management and NRI quota seats — there is no NEET-free admission route at any recognised medical or dental college. Published third-party estimates for annual fees vary enormously by quota and year; treat any number you see online, including on this page, as indicative only and get the current written fee structure directly from the VIMS or VIDS admission office before paying anything.
